## Parties and service

These terms are between [RepairProof legal entity] (the platform operator) and [customer / repair business, as applicable]. RepairProof provides recordkeeping and workflow tools. The repair business remains responsible for inspection, diagnosis, work, parts, pricing, custody, safety, warranty performance, and direct customer obligations. The platform does not itself inspect or repair devices.

## Customer authorization

Before work begins, the customer should be shown the specific repair business, device description, reported concern, diagnosed issue, proposed work, itemized parts and labour, taxes/charges, estimated completion, warranty terms, quotation expiry, and evidence intended to support the estimate. Approval must be an affirmative action tied to the exact quotation version. Any material change in scope or price requires a new version and fresh customer approval. No extracted OCR value or statistical estimate is an approval.

## Records and evidence

Repair records are supplied by customers and repair personnel. RepairProof may preserve versions, timestamps, audit events, and file hashes. A hash indicates byte integrity relative to the recorded digest; it does not prove evidence authenticity, diagnosis truth, parts provenance, or repair quality. Any independent verification must identify the verifier, checklist, scope, evidence reviewed, and limitations.

## Warranty, disputes, and limitations

The actual repair business must state the warranty provider, coverage, term, exclusions, claim route, and response process in the quotation. RepairProof’s workflow record does not replace statutory rights, manufacturer terms, a service contract, or a remedy available under applicable law.
